## 2. Products & Solutions
OQULIX has built 4 key flagship products:

1. **Happy Moves** (VR Physical Therapy & Healthcare)
   * Redefining Physical Therapy. Utilizes cutting-edge Virtual Reality to transform traditional rehabilitation. Immerses patients in interactive, game-like environments to boost motivation and accelerate motor & cognitive recovery. Provides real-time motion tracking and data analytics for therapists.
   * Key Features: Immersive 3D Environments, Real-time Motion Tracking, Gamified Rehabilitation, Clinical Data Analytics.
   * Website: https://www.happymovesrehab.com/

2. **AR Flash Cards** (Augmented Reality Education)
   * Bring Learning to Life. Combines physical flash cards with an augmented reality mobile app. Enables students and children to view 3D models of animals, planets, and historical artifacts popping up interactively.
   * Key Features: Interactive 3D Models, Educational Audio & Text, Multiple Subjects (Science, History), Easy-to-Use App.
   * Website: https://arcards.oqulix.com/

3. **Happy Class** (Smart Board Classroom Platform)
   * The Classroom of the Future. Integrates with interactive smart boards to deliver gamified, curriculum-aligned classroom activities, converting passive lectures into active group learning.
   * Key Features: Smart Board Integration, Multiplayer Activities, Teacher Analytics Dashboard, Customizable Lessons.
   * Website: https://www.oqulix.com/

4. **GAMEFAKTORY** (Interactive Media & Brand Gamification)
   * Turning Engagement Into Game Play / Next-Level Engagement. Delivers custom interactive games and gamified marketing activations for brands, retail, and corporate events to drive audience engagement and loyalty.
   * Key Features: Custom Brand Integration, High-End VR Experiences, Reward-Based Mechanics, Event & Retail Deployment, Leaderboards, Multiplayer Experiences.
   * Website: https://www.gamefaktory.com/
