# 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

Oqulix Private Limited is a Kerala-based technology company focused on building immersive and interactive technology products that combine **Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), 3D applications, gamification, and software platforms**.

Oqulix works across several application areas, including:

* Healthcare and rehabilitation
* Physiotherapy and neuro-rehabilitation

* Augmented reality experiences
* Brand engagement and gamification
* Interactive marketing campaigns
* Entertainment
* 3D applications
* Real-estate visualization
* Training simulations
* Product visualization
* Interactive 360° experiences
* Custom immersive technology solutions

The company's public website describes Oqulix as a technology company providing VR software solutions for businesses and individuals, with a focus on healthcare, learning, customized innovation, and immersive experiences.

Oqulix was incorporated in Kerala in November 2023 and operates from the Ernakulam/Aluva region.

The company is associated with Kerala's startup ecosystem and has worked with healthcare organizations, educational initiatives, brands, and other organizations requiring immersive or interactive digital experiences.

* Player records
* Score validation

* QR token validation
* Cloud Functions
* Cost optimization

For larger campaigns, architecture should prioritize minimizing unnecessary Firestore reads/writes and keeping gameplay state client-side where safe while validating important competitive/reward events server-side.

# 19. GAMEFAKTORY SECURITY / ANTI-CHEAT

Because rewards may depend on game scores, score integrity is important.

The GameFaktory architecture has considered mechanisms such as:

* Server-side score validation
* Firebase Cloud Functions
* Suspicious-account detection
* Player tokens
* QR tokens
* Expiring campaign tokens
* Separation between gameplay state and reward validation

One internal architecture used QR tokens with approximately 30-day expiry and Cloud Functions logic for suspicious-account checks.

The general principle is:

**The client should never be trusted as the sole authority for reward eligibility or high-value scores.**
